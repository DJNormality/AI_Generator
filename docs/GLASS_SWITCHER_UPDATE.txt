Explorer Switcher: all currently open Explorer entries appear as a zoomed-out diagonal stack of icon cards inside the main window. Click Switcher, or Tab outside editable fields (Ctrl+Tab also opens it). Click a card to open. Tab/arrows cycle, Enter opens, Escape cancels. Hold Shift and scroll anywhere in the app; after 650ms without scrolling the selected tool opens. Closed dock entries are excluded. Tools continue to launch inside the main window. Cards show icons and tool names, not live screenshots.

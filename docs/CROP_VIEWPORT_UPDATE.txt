Manual Crop Editor and Guide Crop / Slice Sheet now have a Line Mode button.
Click corners in sequence, then click the first point to close the outline.
Open outlines cannot be saved. Closing only finishes the selection; it does not save automatically.
Save Line Crop exports a PNG at source-pixel resolution with transparency outside the outline.
Escape discards the current line selection and exits Line Mode. It does not close the entire editor.
Backspace or Undo Corner lets you correct the outline. Existing guide and rectangular crop tools remain available.
